Debris we can quote from a description

Drywall scraps, wood, cabinets, flooring, and fixtures from a kitchen or bath update. Bagged or piled is fine. Tell us the volume and whether it is upstairs or across a lawn.

Heavy material needs its own description

Concrete, brick, roofing, dirt, and fill are not the same load as drywall. Describe them before we quote. We will tell you whether that material is part of the job.

Asbestos and hazardous waste

We do not haul asbestos, hazardous waste, fuel, or liquids. Older buildings can involve materials that need special handling. We do not do that handling. Paint and chemicals belong at the city's household chemical drop-off.

Springfield disposal context for debris

If you haul debris yourself, the City of Springfield publishes Material Know How and Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill. Those pages describe landfill limits and examples of clean fill, such as uncontaminated soil, concrete, and brick, and they say facility staff verify what they will accept. Hours and fees change. We are not the landfill, and a city tipping fee is not our price.

Mixed remodel debris, including drywall, lumber, and roofing, is a different load from clean fill. Describe shingles, sheet rock, and mixed construction waste as mixed debris. More city links are on the disposal guide. What changes a quote.
