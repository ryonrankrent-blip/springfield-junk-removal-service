What a storage unit cleanout is

A storage unit cleanout is a haul from a rented unit, not from a house. People book it when a unit has to be empty before a payment deadline, after a move that never got unpacked, or when a relative's unit is still full. The job is the contents. We do not rent units, and we are not the storage company. Call (417) 242-5370 and describe what you can see when the door opens.

Springfield has indoor hallway buildings and rows of drive-up doors, including along the commercial strips and near the colleges. The building type changes the work more than the neighborhood name. A drive-up door lets us load closer to the truck. An indoor unit can mean a freight elevator, a long corridor, and a facility rule about how long a truck may sit at the curb. Tell us which one you have.

Unit size

A 5-by-5 locker, a 5-by-10 closet, a 10-by-10 room, and a 10-by-20 or 10-by-30 bay are different volumes. The size painted on the door is more useful than "pretty full." If the unit is packed to the ceiling, say that too. Height is part of the load.

What is usually inside

Couches, mattresses, dressers, boxes, lamps, and holiday bins. A single piece can be furniture removal or mattress removal. A unit of mixed leftovers is this page. Appliances belong on the list if a refrigerator or washer is in there.

What you keep

Papers, photos, and anything you still want come out with you before we load, or you point them out while we are there. We do not sort a unit for value, and we do not appraise furniture.

Facility rules we need before we quote

Storage companies set their own hours, truck rules, and insurance requirements for vendors. We follow the rules you pass on, and we will tell you if a rule changes whether we can do the job. Ask the office, then tell us:

  • The gate code, lock code, or whether someone from the office has to walk us in.
  • The hours a truck is allowed, and whether weekends are different.
  • Drive-up door or indoor hallway, and which floor.
  • How far we carry items to a legal place to park.
  • Whether the facility requires the unit to be swept or the lock left a certain way. Sweeping the dust out is not part of a junk haul unless we include it in the quote.

We do not cut locks. If the facility has already cut the lock for an auction or a lien process, the person who books the haul needs the right to empty the unit. We do not decide who owns the contents.

Cutoff dates

If the office gave you a date, put that date in the message. We will tell you the first opening we have. Same-day is sometimes possible and is not a promise. A form does not hold the unit, and it does not pause the facility's clock. If you are buying a unit at auction, say so. The person who meets us still needs the right to empty it.

Pricing follows the volume, the weight, and the carry, the same way a house or garage quote does. A mattress or an appliance on top of boxes is an add-on you should name. We do not publish a price per unit size.

Related cleanouts

A garage at the house is a garage cleanout. Stored junk under the house is a basement cleanout. A pull-down stair full of boxes is an attic cleanout. The hub for all of those is property cleanouts.

Items that stay out of a storage haul

Paint, fuel, solvents, ammunition, and anything leaking stay in the unit or go to a drop-off that accepts them. Springfield's household chemical options are on the pickup or drop-off page. We will not haul a unit we cannot see. If you are out of town, someone with the code needs to be there, or you need to tell us how the facility will let us in and what is marked to go. Photos of the open door help the quote match the pile.

Usable furniture is not automatically a donation. If you want a charity to take something, call that organization and confirm before pickup day. We haul what is left. We are not a donation center, and we do not promise a recycling outcome for mattresses left in a unit.
