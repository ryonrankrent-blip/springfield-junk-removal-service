What an estate cleanout means here

Families call when a Springfield house holds a lifetime of furniture, boxes, and household goods, and someone has to decide what leaves. We take the items you mark. We do not buy belongings, price them, or act as an estate-sale company. Call (417) 242-5370 and describe the house.

You decide what stays

Photos, papers, and specific furniture often stay with the family. Walk the rooms with us, or leave notes on what goes. A normal move, with no estate sorting, fits the house cleanout page better.

Several rooms, not one piece

Estate jobs usually cover more than one room, plus a garage, attic, or basement when you include it. One or two pieces, with the house otherwise staying as it is, can go on the residential junk page.

Donation is a separate call

If you want usable items donated, contact that organization yourself before pickup day and confirm they will take them. We are not a donation center. Unwanted leftovers can go on our truck.

What we need before an estate quote

Tell us who will let us in, about how full the rooms are, and whether a garage or basement is part of the haul. Stairs and parking matter. Hazardous waste, asbestos, fuel, ammunition, and medical waste are not part of the job. Rental and apartment work is listed on the cleanouts page, including the apartment cleanout page when the property is a unit rather than a family house.

Questions

Do you do estate cleanouts in Springfield, MO?

Yes. We haul furniture and household belongings you mark after a downsizing or an estate in Springfield. We do not appraise items or run an estate sale.

Will you decide what to keep in an estate?

No. You, or someone you choose, show us what goes and what stays with the family. We haul the items marked to go.

Is an estate cleanout the same as a house cleanout?

No. An estate cleanout is the haul while a family is sorting a house. A house cleanout empties a home for a move or a vacant rental without that sorting. Both start with a call to (417) 242-5370.
