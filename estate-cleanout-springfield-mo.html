For families and the people helping them

An estate cleanout in Springfield is the haul after someone has started deciding what leaves a house. That person might be a relative, an executor handling the practical work, or a realtor who needs the rooms empty before photos or a closing. We take furniture, boxes, and household goods that will not be kept and that a donation stop will not accept. Call (417) 242-5370 and describe the house. We do not buy belongings, price them, or act as an estate-sale company.

The first answer people need is simple. We load and haul. We do not tell you what a chair is worth, and we do not decide which photos stay in the family. Walk the rooms with us, leave notes, or have one person on site who can answer "that goes." If the house is a normal move with no sorting, the house cleanout page is the better fit.

You decide what stays

Papers, jewelry, and specific furniture often stay with the family. Pull those before pickup day if you can. Anything still in a "maybe" pile should be out of the rooms we are clearing, or we will ask and wait. We would rather pause than haul the wrong box.

Several rooms, not one piece

Estate jobs usually cover more than one room, plus a garage, attic, or basement when you include it. One or two pieces, with the house otherwise staying as it is, can go on the residential junk page. Name each floor so the volume in the quote is the volume in the house.

Apartments and heavy clutter

A unit rather than a house is an apartment cleanout. Rooms packed so tightly that pathways are blocked are described on the hoarder cleanout page. That work is still a haul of ordinary household junk. It is not biohazard cleanup.

Donation, city recycling, and what we haul

Keep, donate, then haul is the order that wastes the least. We are not a donation center, and we are not affiliated with any charity or with the City of Springfield. If you want usable furniture or building materials donated, call the organization and confirm they will take those items before our truck is there.

  • Habitat for Humanity of Springfield ReStore accepts many household goods and building materials when the items are in condition their staff will resell. They decide at the door. They are not our partner.
  • Victory Mission publishes a donation list centered on food, clothing, and hygiene items. Call them before you assume a piece of furniture qualifies. The city's mattress program has named Victory Mission as a contractor on the city page. That is a city contract, not an arrangement with us.
  • City drop-off is a separate choice if someone in the family can haul items. Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill, the recycling centers, and mattress recycling publish their own hours and fees. The mattress page has listed $20 per mattress or box spring. Check it. That fee is not our price.

What will not be kept and will not be accepted for donation is what we haul. We do not promise a recycling outcome, and we do not issue a certificate. More city links are on the pickup or drop-off page.

What this page is not

We do not clean for a showing, patch walls, or mow. We do not give legal advice about wills, notices, or who owns an item. Say which situation you are in so the right person is on site when we arrive.
