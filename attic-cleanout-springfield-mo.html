Attic cleanouts start with the opening

An attic cleanout is the carry down, then the haul. The opening decides what is possible. A fixed stair, like a real staircase to a finished or semi-finished attic, is closer to moving items between floors. A pull-down ladder in a hallway is narrower, steeper, and often the only way in. A scuttle hatch with no stair is harder still. Call (417) 242-5370 and name which one the house has. If the ladder is broken, stuck, or missing, say that. We do not install a new stair.

Older Springfield houses often store luggage, holiday decorations, and a spare bed above the ceiling. Newer houses may have a truss attic with no floor, only joists and insulation. We need a surface that can be walked. If the "floor" is joists with insulation between them, tell us. We will not step through a ceiling to reach a box.

What we carry down

Boxes, bins, luggage, small furniture, bagged clothing you are discarding, and holiday items you do not want. Tell us if a dresser, a table, or a headboard has to be taken apart to fit the opening. We quote the pieces you name.

Heat, dust, and low clearance

A Springfield attic in summer is hot, and the work is slower. Low headroom and knee walls mean we cannot stand everywhere. A description of how far you can walk from the opening helps more than a guess at the square footage.

What we leave

Insulation, roof decking, wiring, and bathroom fans stay. We are not an insulation crew or a roofer. Belongings you still want can be moved to the side if you mark them before we arrive.

Furniture that will not fit the hatch

A bookcase or a tabletop that went up in pieces can come down in pieces if the fasteners still work. A solid piece wider than the opening cannot. Measure the opening if you can, or send a photo of the stair and of the largest item. We would rather quote a disassembly than discover it on site and stop. One couch or dresser that is already on a living-room floor is furniture removal, not an attic job. The attic page is for items that are still up there.

Mattresses stored in an attic can go on the same haul. Name the size. If the mattress is the only item and it is already downstairs, use mattress removal. City mattress recycling, if you haul it yourself, is a separate fee on the city's page, linked from the pickup or drop-off page.

Insulation and materials we will not bag

We do not vacuum, remove, or bag attic insulation. Some older insulation materials are not ordinary trash. If you suspect asbestos, leave it alone and do not have us carry it out with the boxes. We do not take asbestos. The same rule covers vermiculite or any loose fill you cannot identify. Point it out so we stay out of that area. Paint, pesticides, and fuel do not belong in an attic haul either.

The quote is the volume of belongings, the weight, and the difficulty of the ladder or stair. There is no attic flat rate. See what changes a junk removal quote. We do not promise same-day service. Call with the day you hope for and we will tell you the first opening.

Garages, basements, and attics are the three places Springfield houses hide storage. The garage cleanout page is the bay at ground level. The basement cleanout page is below grade. A unit you rent off site is a storage unit cleanout. If the whole house is being emptied, including the attic, start with house cleanout or estate cleanout and mention the attic so it is not left out of the volume.
