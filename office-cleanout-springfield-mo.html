Office furniture and cubicles, not every commercial load

This page is for an office: a suite, a floor, or a few rooms of desks and panels. A store closeout, a warehouse of mixed junk, or a remodel pile belongs on commercial junk removal. Drywall, lumber, and flooring are construction debris. Call (417) 242-5370 if the load is mostly furniture and workstations and you need them gone so the space can be re-leased or reused.

Springfield offices sit downtown, along Glenstone, Sunshine, Kearney, and in smaller buildings scattered through the city. The address matters less than the path from the suite to the truck. A ground-floor office with a door to the parking lot is a different carry from a downtown suite that requires a freight elevator and a loading dock reservation.

Desks and chairs

Workstations, reception desks, conference tables, task chairs, and side chairs. Particle-board desks that wobble still count. Tell us if a desk is built in or bolted to a wall. Built-ins may be debris rather than furniture. Say which.

Cubicle tear-outs

Panels, tiles, work surfaces, overhead bins, and pedestals. A tear-out means we take the station apart enough to carry it. It does not mean we repair the floor, paint, or remove cable from the ceiling. Name the approximate station count. "The whole floor" is not a count.

Cabinets and paper

Lateral and vertical files are heavy when they are full. We can haul them full if you say so. We do not sort records, and we do not shred. If files must stay for legal reasons, mark the cabinets that stay before we arrive.

Building access in Springfield

Tell us who will let us in. That is usually an office manager, a property manager, or the tenant on the way out. We do not have master keys, and we do not sign a building's vendor packet unless that step is agreed before the day of the haul. Useful details:

  • Floor number, and whether the passenger elevator can be used or only a freight car.
  • Dock hours, a certificate the building asked for, and any after-hours window.
  • Where a truck may park, and for how long.
  • Protection the building wants on floors or elevator walls. We will say if that changes the quote.
  • Whether the suite is still staffed. Working around people is slower than an empty office.

A property-manager turnover of an apartment is not this page. That job is an apartment cleanout or an eviction cleanout.

Electronics and what we will not certify

Monitors, docks, printers, and small computers can go on the list. We haul the hardware. We do not wipe data, destroy drives, or give you a certificate that anything was recycled. Pull drives or clear accounts before pickup if the data matters. Copiers and large printers need their own line in the quote because of weight. We do not take toner spills, batteries loose in a box, or chemicals from a janitor's closet as if they were office junk.

There is no office price list. Volume, weight, stairs or elevator time, and the hours we are allowed in the building set the quote. A mattress left in a break room or a refrigerator in a kitchenette should be named; those add-ons are explained on the cost page. We do not promise same-day service. If a lease ends on a date, tell us that date and we will tell you the first time we can be there.

Where this page stops

Kitchenettes with household appliances can be included if you list them. A full restaurant or a retail fixture pull is commercial junk, not an office cleanout. Construction that has already opened walls is construction debris. If you are clearing a house that happened to contain a desk, use residential junk removal or a house cleanout. The commercial hub remains the right start when the load is mixed and you are not sure which page fits. Call (417) 242-5370 and describe the suite in a few sentences.
