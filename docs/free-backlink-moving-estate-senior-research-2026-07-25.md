## 1. SpringHouse Village

### Exact page-level gap

The live Springfield-focused guide recommends sorting belongings into keep, family, donate, and discard categories and planning donation pickups and estate sales. It does not provide a Springfield-specific lookup path for restricted materials, electronics, appliances, bulky items, household chemicals, or items that cannot be donated.

The Springfield junk disposal guide would add a practical local reference without implying that SpringHouse Village endorses a junk-removal provider. The proposed destination must remain educational and official-source-led.

### Route and risk

The public contact page is designed for sales and community inquiries and requires fields about the prospective resident, move timing, senior-living familiarity, and lifestyle options. That form is not suitable for an editorial-resource suggestion. Sales and community phone numbers are also operational routes and should not be used for a link proposal.

### Recommendation

Keep at **A- / route unresolved**. This is the strongest exact content fit in the batch, but no outreach draft should advance until a genuine editorial, marketing, or community-relations route is verified.

## 2. Help Give Hope

### Exact page-level gap

The nonprofit's garage-sale page actively solicits donations, lists several items it does not take, asks donors to describe appliance condition accurately, and explains that unusable items can create removal costs. It does not provide readers with a neutral Springfield lookup path for excluded items, electronics, mattresses, broken appliances, household chemicals, or other facility-dependent materials.

A carefully framed optional citation to the Springfield junk disposal guide or accepted-items guide could help donors make a better decision before arranging pickup. The proposal must not imply partnership, endorsement, donation acceptance, pickup coordination, or that the nonprofit should send unwanted materials to a commercial service.

### Route and risk

The site has a public general contact form and an email link. The garage-sale phone number is specifically for scheduling donation pickup and must not be used for editorial outreach. Any future draft should address the exact garage-sale page, lead with donor clarity and cost avoidance, and make clear that the resource is optional and free to cite.

### Recommendation

Advance only after a separate route-verification and draft approval. Score **B+** because the local reader benefit is strong, but nonprofit sensitivity and the absence of a named editor require restraint.

## 3. DIYAuctions

### Exact page-level gap

The Springfield estate-sale guide tells readers to plan for donation, consignment, or disposal after the sale and mentions local donation organizations. It does not provide a current Springfield decision path for restricted materials, household chemicals, electronics, appliances, tires, bulky items, or official item-specific disposal lookup.

The Springfield junk disposal guide is a natural supporting resource for the post-sale clean-out section, especially if described as a local item-by-item reference rather than a service recommendation.

### Route and risk

DIYAuctions publishes hundreds of local-market guides and operates a commercial estate-auction platform. Its contact page lists a business email for partnerships, media, and business development, but not a named editor. The page's scale, formulaic local coverage, and promotional purpose reduce the likelihood of a durable independent editorial citation.

### Recommendation

Hold at **B** behind SpringHouse Village and Help Give Hope. Consider one individualized draft only if higher-priority local routes remain unavailable. Do not offer a partnership, reciprocal link, affiliate arrangement, or promotion.

## 4. Veterans Clearance Outlet

### Fit review

The Springfield nonprofit resale store invites estate and household-item donations and provides a general email and donation form. Its public pages do not provide a detailed accepted-items or rejected-items resource comparable to Help Give Hope's garage-sale page.

An accepted-items or disposal-guide citation could theoretically help donors with items the store cannot use, but there is no exact editorial article or demonstrated missing paragraph. The visible form is designed for donation inquiries.

### Recommendation

Defer at **B-**. Reconsider only if the organization publishes an accepted-items, donation-preparation, or estate-donation guide with a clear missing local disposal path. Do not use the donation form merely to seek a backlink.
