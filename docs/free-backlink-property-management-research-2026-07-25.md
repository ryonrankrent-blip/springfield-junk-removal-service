## 1. Springfield Property Management LLC

### Exact gap

The move-out page tells residents to remove all personal property. Its downloadable checklist also says all items and trash must be removed and presents a historical hauling-to-dump charge range. It does not give readers a current Springfield decision path for bulky items, restricted or hazardous materials, donation/recycling lookup, facility-rule verification, or the photos and access details useful for obtaining a removal quote.

### Editorial fit

A short neutral link to the Springfield student/renter move-out checklist or disposal guide could help residents act on the page's removal requirement without representing Springfield Junk Removal Service as a preferred vendor.

### Route and risk

The publisher exposes a general contact form and `info@springfieldpm.com`. The same route is used for operational tenant matters, rental inquiries, notices, and walkthroughs; it is not a verified editorial inbox. Any future draft must be short, identify the exact page, ask only for editorial consideration, and avoid a vendor-list request.

### Recommendation

One owner-approved email was sent once. The 2026-07-28 outcome audit found no reply, delivery failure, or live link. Keep pending and do not resend, use the public form, use another route, or follow up without separate approval.
